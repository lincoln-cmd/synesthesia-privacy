Synesthesia Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026 · Last updated November 4, 2026

Synesthesia respects your privacy. The current app does not provide accounts and does not collect personal data through a developer-operated server.

1. Data processed on your device

The app stores learning progress, answer results, scores, mastery, review schedules, custom practice sets, and learning settings in local app storage. It does not collect your name, email address, or location.

2. Microphone permission and audio processing

The microphone is used only while you start pitch and timing measurement in sight-singing, phrase imitation, or entrance-exam practice. Audio is analyzed on the device. Exercises that offer replay may keep measurement audio temporarily in the app cache; it is deleted when you start a new measurement, move to another question, or leave the screen. Audio and recordings are not sent to the developer or any third party. You can use theory, ear training, music reading, and rhythm features without granting microphone permission.

3. Storage, backup, and retention

Learning history and settings remain in local app storage while you use the app. If Android backup is enabled, learning data may be included in Google Android backup or device-transfer services. Cache and temporary audio are excluded from backup.

4. External transfer and third parties

The current version does not transmit learning data or microphone audio to a developer server or third party. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

5. Deleting your data

Use Learning settings → Reset all learning data to delete scores, mastery, lesson progress, custom practice sets, and learning settings. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app also removes local data. Copies in Android backup are managed through your Google Account backup settings.
